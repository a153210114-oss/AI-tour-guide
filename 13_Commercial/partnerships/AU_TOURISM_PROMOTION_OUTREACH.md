## Pilot proposition

Propose a 60–90 day Great Ocean Road pilot with participating tour companies:

- company assignment selects the driver, route and locked product version;
- multilingual guide assistance and clearly labelled visitor listening modes;
- location use only after driver authorization;
- verified destination knowledge with image-assisted recognition;
- retained, timestamped guide audio with explicit sharing/reward choices;
- operator training and location-triggered approved playback;
- aggregate reporting on usage, language demand, recognition corrections, visitor ratings
  and guide workload, without exposing personal recordings.

## Evidence to prepare before outreach

- Australian business identity and named project owner;
- a working mobile demonstration;
- Great Ocean Road operator willing to participate;
- privacy, recording, location and Indigenous cultural-content review;
- one-page product summary and two-minute demo video;
- pilot objectives, safety boundaries and success measures;
- content/image licence register;
- explanation that live translation is in test mode until its provider is connected.
