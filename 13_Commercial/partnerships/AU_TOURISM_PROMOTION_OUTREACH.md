# Australia Tourism Promotion Outreach

Status: Working Plan  
Verified: 2026-09-06

## Recommended route

Do not begin by asking Tourism Australia for a national endorsement. First establish a
Victoria pilot with measurable visitor and operator outcomes, then take that evidence to
the national body.

1. **Visit Victoria — Industry Engagement & Programs**
   - Best first contact for a new Melbourne/Victoria tourism product.
   - Send product news and a pilot proposal to `product@visitvictoria.com.au`.
   - Ask about the International Mentoring Program, Marketing Excellence Program and
     suitable marketing/PR pathways.
   - Official source: https://corporate.visitvictoria.com/resources/industry-development
   - Industry guide: https://assets-corporate.visitvictoria.com/documents/Working-With-Visit-Victoria-December-2024.pdf

2. **Great Ocean Road Regional Tourism / Visitor Economy Partnership**
   - Best regional pilot partner because the first recognition and route pack covers the
     Great Ocean Road.
   - Official contact directory: https://corporate.visitvictoria.com/resources/our-ecosystem/regional-tourism-boards
   - Ask for introductions to tour operators, a supervised field pilot and regional
     destination-content review.

3. **Australian Tourism Data Warehouse (ATDW) through Visit Victoria**
   - Use after there is an eligible visitor-facing tour/service and required business
     details. ATDW distributes approved operator profiles into official tourism channels.
   - Victorian support: `atdwsupport@visitvictoria.com.au`, `1300 306 366`.
   - Official eligibility: https://corporate.visitvictoria.com/resources/marketing-opportunities/about-atdw/atdw-listings-regional

4. **Tourism Australia — industry partnership pathway**
   - Approach after the Victoria pilot proves export readiness, multilingual visitor value,
     operator adoption and measurable outcomes.
   - Tourism Australia works with state tourism organisations and selected distribution
     partners on international campaigns; a state-backed case is a stronger entry point.
   - Official partnership overview: https://www.tourism.australia.com/en/about/our-organisation/our-partnerships.html

5. **Melbourne Convention Bureau (optional)**
   - Relevant when the product supports conferences, incentive groups or business-event
     touring; it is not the main channel for ordinary leisure-tour promotion.
   - Partnership contact: `partnerships@melbournecb.com.au`.
   - Official program: https://www.melbournecb.com.au/who-we-work-with/our-partnership-program

## Pilot proposition

Propose a 60–90 day Great Ocean Road pilot with participating tour companies:

- company assignment selects the driver, route and locked product version;
- multilingual guide assistance and clearly labelled visitor listening modes;
- location use only after driver authorization;
- verified destination knowledge with image-assisted recognition;
- retained, timestamped guide audio with explicit sharing/reward choices;
- operator training and location-triggered approved playback;
- aggregate reporting on usage, language demand, recognition corrections, visitor ratings
  and guide workload, without exposing personal recordings.

## Evidence to prepare before outreach

- Australian business identity and named project owner;
- a working mobile demonstration;
- Great Ocean Road operator willing to participate;
- privacy, recording, location and Indigenous cultural-content review;
- one-page product summary and two-minute demo video;
- pilot objectives, safety boundaries and success measures;
- content/image licence register;
- explanation that live translation is in test mode until its provider is connected.
