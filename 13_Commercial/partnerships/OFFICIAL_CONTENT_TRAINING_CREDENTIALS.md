# Official Content, Guide Training and Credentials

Status: Partnership Design  
Verified: 2026-09-06

## Partnership value exchange

Tourism bodies and destination partners can provide authoritative destination updates,
public resource links, approved media/content licences, brand guidance and subject-matter
review. AI Tour Guide can turn licensed material into versioned multilingual learning,
route simulations, guide practice, assessments and operational feedback.

Publicly accessible does not automatically mean commercially reusable. Every imported
resource records publisher, URL, retrieval/update time, licence status, attribution and
cultural-review requirement. Unknown rights remain link-only until written permission or
a reusable licence is confirmed. First Peoples stories, names, images and knowledge need
appropriate Traditional Owner/source approval; a tourism-body source alone is not a
blanket cultural licence.

## Credential ladder

1. **AI Tour Guide course completion** — issued by this platform; proves completion only.
2. **Co-branded partner badge** — issued only under a written assessment and branding
   agreement with the named tourism/destination partner.
3. **Industry accreditation** — verified and issued by the relevant professional body,
   such as Tour Guides Australia; the platform may prepare evidence but cannot self-issue it.
4. **National qualification** — delivered/assessed by an authorised Registered Training
   Organisation against the applicable national training package. AI Tour Guide may serve
   as learning delivery or evidence infrastructure under an agreement, not represent itself
   as the qualification issuer unless separately authorised.

No screen may label an internal completion certificate as “Tourism Australia certified”,
“government accredited” or equivalent without an executed issuer agreement and a live
external verification record.

## Relevant official pathways

- Tourism Australia's Aussie Specialist Program trains frontline travel sellers in ten
  languages; it is a useful content/training integration reference, but it is not itself
  professional tour-guide accreditation:
  https://www.tourism.australia.com/en/resources/industry-resources/industry-programs/aussie-specialist-program.html
- Tour Guides Australia manages a national Professional Tour Guide Accreditation Program
  and defines experience, assessment, first-aid and professional requirements:
  https://tga.org.au/join-tga/professional-guide-accreditation/
- Australia's National Training Register publishes current guiding competencies and
  qualifications; delivery and assessment of nationally recognised credentials must follow
  the registered training framework:
  https://training.gov.au/Training/Details/SITTGDE014
- Visit Victoria's Industry Development team is the first proposed partner for a Victorian
  official-content and guide-capability pilot:
  https://corporate.visitvictoria.com/resources/industry-development

## Proposed Victoria pilot

- Obtain written permission for selected official destination facts, safety updates,
  maps, images and brand assets.
- Create a Great Ocean Road learning pathway with Mandarin and English delivery.
- Assess factual accuracy, route safety, visitor communication, cultural respect,
  emergency handling and practical commentary.
- Issue an AI Tour Guide completion record for the pilot.
- Invite Visit Victoria/Great Ocean Road Regional Tourism to review content and explore a
  co-branded badge without pre-claiming endorsement.
- Map learning evidence toward Tour Guides Australia accreditation and/or an RTO pathway;
  the external body retains assessment and issuing authority.
- Feed verified real-tour performance back into private coaching and company training,
  with contributor permissions and rewards preserved.
