# Guide External Assessment Evidence

Status: Governance Baseline

## Purpose

With the guide's explicit permission, verified real-tour audio and visitor feedback may be
assembled as supporting evidence for an excellence award, professional accreditation,
company promotion or tourism-body review. AI Tour Guide prepares evidence; the external
body owns its assessment framework, decision and credential.

## Evidence bundle

The bundle can include:

- references to untouched original audio and transcripts;
- exact tour, route, place and time context;
- verified-tour count and distinct visitor-group count;
- rating distribution, not only an average score;
- complaints and independently upheld complaints;
- factual correction rate and content reuse signals;
- languages and route complexity;
- safety events and operational compliance;
- guide consent and a SHA-256 integrity manifest.

## Fairness and anti-gaming

- Only feedback tied to a verified tour may enter the assessment summary.
- Duplicate, incentivised, company-staff and suspicious ratings must be flagged or excluded.
- Audio samples should cover multiple tours and situations rather than a single best clip.
- Ratings must be interpreted alongside route difficulty, language, group type and sample size.
- A poor rating is not proof of misconduct; complaints require a separate review process.
- Guides can inspect their bundle, correct factual metadata, add context and appeal a decision.
- Private coaching data is not sent externally unless included in the guide's specific consent.
- Visitor identity and unnecessary conversation are removed or access-restricted before review.

## Claims and issuing authority

The platform may display “evidence prepared for review” after integrity checks. It must not
display “tourism-board approved”, “excellent guide” or “accredited” until the named external
organisation returns a signed/verifiable decision under a stated framework version.

The issuing organisation, assessor, decision time, outcome and verification URL are retained
with the credential. Revocation or expiry from the issuer must propagate to the guide profile.
