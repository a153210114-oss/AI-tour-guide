# Knowledge Packs

`melbourne_knowledge_seed_r1.json` is a researched seed, not a finished narration pack.

Rules:

- Stable claims and dynamic visitor information remain separate.
- Every claim keeps its source, publisher, grade and verification date.
- First Peoples knowledge requires cultural authority and pronunciation review.
- Recognition images live under `03_Recognition_Matching/images`; image licences and
  attribution are tracked separately from factual sources.
- A passing schema or unit test does not make the pack production-approved.
