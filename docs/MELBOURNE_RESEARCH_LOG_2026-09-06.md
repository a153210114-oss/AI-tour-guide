# Melbourne Knowledge and Recognition Research Log

## Scope

Greater Melbourne, central visitor routes and common day-trip regions. Audience is the
AI Tour Guide knowledge and recognition pipeline. The first pass prioritised official
government, museum, library, cultural institution and destination authority sources.

## Result

- 39 sourced knowledge/route nodes in `melbourne_knowledge_seed_r1.json`.
- Ten locally downloaded, checksummed recognition reference images across eight places.
- Flinders Street Station now has three inspected views covering entrance clocks,
  upper facade and clock-tower facade; all three are clear daytime images, so the place
  remains below the recognition-readiness diversity gate.
- Additional landmark categories remain a rights-review queue, not an approved dataset.

## Material limitations

- “All web” is an open-ended corpus, so this is a versioned first research batch rather
  than a claim of exhaustive internet coverage.
- Opening hours, prices, closures, transport and wildlife sightings are dynamic and need
  runtime checks.
- First Peoples material is not approved for public narration until reviewed by the
  relevant Traditional Owner authority.
- A few public attractions sources are authoritative destination summaries, not deep
  historical monographs; later passes should add archival corroboration where needed.
- CC BY-SA images are approved here for internal research; commercial training and the
  share-alike effect on model artefacts require legal review.

## Stop reason

The first pass reached broad route/theme coverage and primary support for each included
claim. Further broad searching now has lower value than targeted node enrichment,
coordinates, field photography and cultural/rights review.
