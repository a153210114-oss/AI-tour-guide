# Melbourne Knowledge Collection — Canonical Research Report

Audience: AI Tour Guide product and content team  
Research date: 2026-09-06  
Scope: Greater Melbourne city routes, landmark recognition, history, culture, nature,
and common day-trip regions.

## Executive result

The current seed contains 39 sourced nodes and a 10-place visual-recognition catalog.
Ten reusable reference images have been downloaded with file-page provenance, licence,
author and SHA-256 integrity records. This is broad first-pass coverage, not exhaustive
internet capture and not a production-approved narration pack.

## Evidence synthesis

Central Melbourne can be organised into five high-value guide routes: lanes and arcades,
architecture, Aboriginal Melbourne, parks and gardens, and the Birrarung/Yarra waterfront.
The wider destination layer adds Port Phillip Bay, Dandenong/Yarra Ranges, Mornington
Peninsula, Phillip Island and Great Ocean Road corridors.

Historic interpretation should connect continuous First Peoples Country, colonial survey
and dispossession, the gold-rush transformation, migration, federation, civic institutions
and modern cultural development. First Peoples knowledge requires cultural authority and
pronunciation review before narration.

## Limitations and disagreements

Dynamic claims are explicitly marked. Destination-authority summaries are useful for
route scope but do not replace archive-level research. Share-alike images are suitable for
internal research subject to attribution; commercial model-training implications require
legal review.

## Verification

The source pack, recognition catalog and image manifest parse as valid JSON. Automated
checks verify minimum coverage, required source fields, local image existence and hashes.
The coverage report also prevents a place being marked recognition-ready without enough
variation in scale, weather and daypart.
